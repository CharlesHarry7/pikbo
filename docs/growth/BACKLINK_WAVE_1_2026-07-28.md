## Evidence rules

- `submitted`: the final form was sent, but review was not confirmed.
- `pending`: the target explicitly confirmed that the listing is under review.
- `published`: a public Pikbo listing URL is accessible.
- `verified_backlink`: the public listing contains a crawlable link to Pikbo and
  that link was rechecked.
- `requires_owner`: progress stopped because the next step needs login, CAPTCHA,
  payment, email verification, a personal identity choice, or action-time
  approval for an external submission.

Filling a draft is not a submission. A submission is not a published listing,
and a published listing is not a verified backlink until its public URL and
outbound link are both checked.
