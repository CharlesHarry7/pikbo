# Pikbo product north star

Pikbo's long-term category is a designer-toy-native AI content platform. The
current product is deliberately smaller:

> One owned toy photo becomes one directed, private, publishable video Moment.

The first Moment is **Street Power-Up**. Pikbo wins by making the product easier
for a toy seller than a generic model interface: a visual preset, no prompt or
model hunting, strong product-identity preservation, private delivery, reliable
recovery, and a clear next-SKU loop.

Higgsfield is a reference for product mechanics—preset selection, fast proof,
projects, reuse, and payment—not a requirement to copy its interface or expose
its breadth before Pikbo's core works.

## Priority order

1. Toy remains recognizable and commercially truthful.
2. Seller reaches a downloadable result quickly.
3. Result survives refresh and can be reused for the next SKU.
4. Accounting, privacy, and cost are correct.
5. A target seller is willing to pay and use it again.

## Not now

Three-video Packs, generic generation controls, Explore, Community, Cinema,
batch tools, many models, mass SEO pages, and broad creator-marketplace ideas
are frozen. They may return only after the single-Moment workflow demonstrates
real quality, repeat use, and sustainable margin.
