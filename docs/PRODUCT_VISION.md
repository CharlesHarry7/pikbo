# Pikbo Product Vision

**Updated:** 2026-08-05
**Vision:** Pikbo.ai = Higgsfield.ai for Designer Toys (潮玩)

## Why This Vision

Higgsfield.ai proved that an AI video generation platform with:
- Viral presets (50+ one-click effects)
- Public community (prompts + assets visible)
- Supercomputer/Apps ecosystem
- Creator tools and contests

...can build a massive engaged user base. Pikbo applies the same playbook
to the **designer toy / art toy (潮玩)** vertical — a space with passionate
collectors, creators, and sellers who need AI video tools purpose-built for toys.

## Core Differentiator

Pikbo is NOT a generic AI video tool. Every feature is toy-first:
- **Effects** designed for toy aesthetics (unboxing, 360 spin, battle scene, etc.)
- **Community** shows toy collections and videos with prompts
- **Studio** tools for toy sellers and collectors
- **Presets** that understand toy proportions, materials, and packaging

## Target Users

1. **Toy Sellers/Brands** — need marketing videos for their products
2. **Toy Collectors** — want to showcase their collection creatively
3. **Toy Designers** — prototype and visualize new designs
4. **Toy Content Creators** — make viral toy content

## Product Philosophy

- **Show, don't tell** — every feature has visible output (videos, not settings)
- **Community first** — all prompts and assets are public by default
- **Toy-native** — effects, presets, and tools are purpose-built for toys
- **Premium quality** — Founding Studio at $49/month for serious creators
- **Fast iteration** — one-click effects beat complex workflows

## Immediate Priorities (Post Private Beta)

1. **Toy Effect Studio** — build out 20+ toy-specific effects (Action Figure, Unboxing, 360 Spin, Battle, Diorama, Stop Motion, Glow Up, etc.)
2. **Community Showcase** — public feed of toy videos with prompts/assets
3. **Explore Page** — discover effects, toys, creators
4. **Pricing Page** — Founding Studio + future tiers
5. **Onboarding Flow** — sign up → first toy video in < 2 minutes

## What NOT to Build

- Generic AI video tools (not differentiated from Higgsfield/Runway)
- Enterprise/team features (not yet)
- Mobile apps (web-first until PMF)
- Marketplace/e-commerce (focus on creation, not transactions)
