## Evidence

`evidence/` contains dated technical and browser proof. Evidence records what
was tested at that time; it does not authorize a feature or production change.

## Historical material

All other product plans, audits, growth reports, screenshots, prompts, sprint
documents, and handoff logs are historical unless a Current document links to
them explicitly. Terms such as Seller Pack, Explore, Community, Cinema,
Supercomputer, Creator/Shop plans, multi-model studio, and mass SEO describe
previous directions and must not be revived from those files.

Git history preserves every superseded document. Do not duplicate historical
material into a new current plan.
