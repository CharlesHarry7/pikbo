# Xiaohongshu toy-visual research — 2026-07-29

Search terms: `潮玩`, `潮玩摄影`, `潮玩设计`, `潮玩展陈`.

This was a read-only composition and art-direction study of public results in
the user's logged-in browser. About 117 search results were scanned and 12
representative posts were opened. Pikbo did not download, embed, trace, or
reproduce any post image, author copy, brand asset, or existing character.

## Findings translated into Pikbo rules

1. **One protagonist before a gallery.** Strong posts establish a recognizable
   silhouette and emotion before showing variants.
2. **Give the toy a small world.** Rooms, rain, wood, paper sets and practical
   props communicate story better than an abstract neon background.
3. **Natural light sells material.** Window light, warm side light, overcast
   reflections and shallow depth make vinyl and handmade surfaces credible.
4. **Series coherence beats variety.** Keep character, material, props and
   palette stable; change only the story variable for each Recipe.
5. **Show making as proof.** A phone input, behind-the-scenes set and separate
   cached output explain the product without pretending an editorial image is a
   model result.
6. **Use restrained graphics.** Low-saturation scenes plus one accent preserve
   both copy legibility and the character silhouette.
7. **Design distance in layers.** Hero establishes one distant focal point;
   Recipe and Project reveal detail and evidence later.

## Originality boundary

Pikbo's v2 protagonist uses an original asymmetrical pebble/fin body, dot eyes,
diagonal brow, matte ivory vinyl and one lime wrist tag. It must not acquire
recognizable ears, teeth, eye shapes, proportions, garments, names, or poses
from Labubu, Hirono, DIMOO, or another known IP. Research affects only
composition, lighting, evidence structure, and scene storytelling.

The implemented decision and scorecard live in
`docs/proof/wave-a-visual-v2/candidates/candidate-review.md`; file hashes and
truth labels live in `docs/ASSETS.md`.
